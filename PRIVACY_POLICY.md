# Privacy Policy for Focus Frog

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

**Focus Frog** ("the App") is developed by **kiraak-h** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
Focus Frog is designed as a **privacy-first, offline-first application**:
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, IP address, device ID, or location.
- **Local Storage Only:** All application data—including focus session history, streak counters, bug currency balance, and equipped accessories—is stored locally on your device using Android's Room Database and DataStore Preferences.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** The App contains no advertising networks, SDK analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK), or third-party tracking scripts.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
Focus Frog requests only essential Android permissions required for core app features:
- **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Used solely to display local notifications when a focus session completes and to deliver scheduled daily evening reminders (if enabled by you).
- **`VIBRATE`**: Used solely to provide subtle haptic feedback during timer interactions and session completion.

None of these permissions are used to collect or export data from your device.

---

### 3. Children's Privacy
Focus Frog is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, the App complies with child privacy protections, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

---

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
